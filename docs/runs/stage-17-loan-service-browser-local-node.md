# Stage 17 in browsers on the local node: a ParyonUSD loan carried out from the wallet's screens with Paryon's loan service, through the loss of a phone

Written by `scripts/stage-17-loan-service-browser.ts --within 12 --screens` on 2026-11-22 01:29 UTC. Chromium (Playwright), one browser profile per device kept on disk under `.local-runs/stage-17-loan-service-browser/`. The pages are this checkout's production builds (`deploy/preview.sh build`) served by `deploy/serve.mjs` with the content security policy at http://localhost:8361/, http://localhost:8362/ and http://localhost:8364/, against the regtest node, with two backup stores and a relay for the sessions between the pages, all on this machine. Paryon's contracts as published were deployed on the local chain by the run (the price at $208.70, periods of 10 blocks) and named to the loan page and to Paryon's service app in their configuration. The service app (`apps/paryon-service`) ran from its command line on 127.0.0.1:8366, with the same configuration file as the pages; the loan page's server passed `/service/` to it (`--forward`), so that the template's service is at http://localhost:8364/service/v1, beside the loan page, as in production. The wallet page was allowed to connect to the loan page's origin (`--connect http://localhost:8364`), and gave the template's service to its client as one of a chain of its own (`local`, where an `http:` address is allowed). The loan page only offered the template; with its tab closed, every action but one was carried out from the wallet's own screens. Times in the log are seconds since the start.
